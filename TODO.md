- [ ] **Warn when a deck's theme isn't found.** A deck that says `theme: chems` with no matching theme file renders silently with Marp's default theme (it lost its `.two` columns in `12. Partial Differential Equations.marp`). imarp now looks for `chems.css` or `theme.css`, beside the deck or in `assets/`, and re-renders when the theme file is added or changes; what's left is telling the presenter when none of them is there (or its `@theme` name doesn't match), instead of silently falling back.

The iframe part has never been tested. Test slides 3 to 6 of `iCloud/iMarp/Markdown test/iMarp control test.md`.
- [ ] **Widgets don't redraw when the slide is small and zoomed** (iPad). With the window at half the screen (the slide drawn at about 0.48 scale) and the slide pinch-zoomed, a widget works (the log shows the Pencil on Plotly's slider handle and the slider stepping) but the screen keeps the old picture until the zoom ends. Full screen it's fine. Cause: WebKit mishandles iframes inside Marp's scaled slide (each slide's `<section>` is scaled inside an SVG `foreignObject`, and WebKit applies that scale twice: `getBoundingClientRect` is off by the scale, which is why `reportFrameRects` now measures in slide units). Nudging the iframe's opacity each frame didn't make it redraw. Fix: move each slide's iframes out of the SVG into a layer over the slides on the iPad too, as the `mac-trackpad` branch does on the Mac (`liftFrame`); then `reportFrameRects` can go back to plain `getBoundingClientRect`. Test on slide 18 of `12. Partial Differential Equations` at half screen.
